# Participant contribution and exposure (0.1)

Informative. Demand as Architecture 0.3 already identifies what participating actors hold and what small contribution each may make. This table adds the reciprocal exposure account. Disclosure is not presumed harmful; it is stated so a holder can decide.

| Actor | Bounded contribution | Possible exposure | Holder control | Intended benefit | Not requested |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Homeowner / owner | Declared need; guided-manual opening measurements; consent | Place-specific opening and project intent | Owner holds declaration and observations; shareState stays private until an explicit later grant | A fitted simulation packet for one alcove | Customer lists, household finances, other rooms |
| Capture actor | Measurement procedure and station labels | That a named actor measured a place | Observation provenance remains attributable and withdrawable with the project | Traceable measurements | Continuous location history |
| Local yard / shop | Later: disclosed machine envelope limits and refused operations | Equipment or service limits | Envelope remains holder-controlled; M1 uses a fixture envelope only | Knowing whether a simulated packet is even discussable | Prices, margins, schedules, customer allocation |
| Contractor | Later: recurring field burdens and install constraints | Trade practice and typical site burdens | Contractor scope stays role-bounded | Fewer surprise fit failures | Client lists, bids, crew identity beyond a role |
| College / training shop | Later: training envelope and student-role limits | Training or equipment boundaries | Institutional envelope and roster stay with the institution | Practice on simulated packets | Student records |
| Manufacturer | Later: material class limits and documented dimensions | Material or dimensional limits | Spec remains a class/spec, not a SKU graph | Correct blank sizing language | Formulations, process recipes |
| 3D Solutions | Assumptions, interfaces, refusal logic, simulation policy | How the system refuses and what it will not compute | Rules stay versioned and reviewable; no automatic rule change from observations | A governable simulation path | Unrestricted reuse of private refusals |

No prices, customer allocation, rankings, customer lists, process recipes, margins, production schedules, or confidential client information are requested.
