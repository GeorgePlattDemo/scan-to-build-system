Why are we allowed to use this fact?

Outdoor Build does not begin with a drawing. It begins by deciding which facts are allowed to control the drawing. The research dossier supplies candidates. This page curates the authority rules that determine what may become usable project geometry.

01

The rules that survive every family.

These are the controls that keep a simple build surface from turning convenience into fake precision.

FAMILY PRECEDES GEOMETRY.

A 25° 2×6 A-frame leg and a 30° 2×8 A-frame leg are separate named systems unless a verified transform relates them.

DATUM IS PART OF THE DIMENSION.

“32¾ in” is incomplete without saying that Home Depot measures the finished leg along each outer edge.

ANGLE CONVENTION IS PART OF THE FACT.

30° does not tell you whether the cut is off-square, a bevel, which face carries it, or whether the ends are parallel.

STATE STAYS ATTACHED.

SUPPORTED, DERIVED, PROJECT-SPECIFIC, INFERRED and UNKNOWN are not cosmetic labels; they determine what may control.

UNKNOWN REMAINS UNKNOWN.

A picture of an X does not establish a crossing coordinate. An assembly photo does not create a bolt-center datum.

STRUCTURAL RELATIONSHIP CHANGES CAN DEFER.

If an edit moves an intersection, brace, leg angle, support relationship or load path outside verified evidence, the honest result may be DEFER.

03

Three compact contrasts worth carrying into the product.

The research is deep; the lesson can still be short.

30° ≠ UNIVERSAL A-FRAME

30° ≠ “standard”
Home Depot: 30° · 2×8 · parallel ends · 32¾ along edge.
This Old House: 25° · 2×6 PT · parallel cuts · 31½ rough cut-list leg.

Do not average them into a generic picnic-table leg.

30° ≠ COMPLETE CUT DEFINITION

30° + direction
X Bench class A: 30° · ends parallel.
X Bench class B: 30° · ends not parallel.

Same angle number. Different part.

MITER ≠ BEVEL

10° ≠ 10°
Sawhorse end leg: 10° double bevel.
Other member: 10° angle off square.

The saw orientation and resulting geometry are different facts.

05

Authority stops before Store and machine authority begins.

The project definition can become trustworthy without pretending to own inventory, process capability or physical release.

  • which family a fact belongs to
  • named design/version
  • published part geometry
  • derived values with visible inputs
  • project-specific measurement requirements
  • unknown / defer conditions

THIS PAGE DOES NOT CREATE

  • actual stock identity / treatment
  • inventory or allocation
  • machine/cell capability
  • kerf / tooling / postprocessor facts
  • payment or commercial acceptance
  • machine readiness, release or Cycle Start
