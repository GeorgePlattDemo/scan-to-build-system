Material

Tower sides shelf pins
Dividers centered

A small starter mark, 3/16 in wide and 3/16 in deep, that you drill through. Tower sides: one on each side board at every shelf height, 1½ or 2 in from the edge, so the shelf pins line up and the shelves sit level. Dividers: one centered where each divider meets the board above and below it, to place its fasteners. The Store spots and prices each one.
