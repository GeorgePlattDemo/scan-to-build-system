DEV GUIDE

Keep the source. Don’t overwrite it with extracted facts.

The customer’s drawing controls intent. Translation may not redesign it.

Source ≠ status. Where a number came from is separate from whether it controls.

No silent promotion. A number in an image stays context until the holder says it controls.

Conflicts stay visible. Don’t average, round away, or pick a winner.

Block only where the definition depends on the gap.

No engineering at intake: no span rules, load claims or fastener advice.

Chain of custody: source → observation → choice → definition.

DEV GUIDE

The customer’s elevation governs. The bench translates it; it doesn’t redesign it.

Three top-level modules. Don’t invent a fourth to make the code easier.

Enter a number once. Everything downstream derives from the same revision.

Part ≠ board. A 14 in deep shelf is two boards across. Keep both truths.

Solid ¾ in board only. No plywood, MDF or glued-panel shortcut.

No joinery, spans or loads invented. The app is not a substitute engineer.

Intent before inventory. The bench says what’s wanted; the Store says what it can do.

A depth that isn’t a board width is edge-milled: the whole board, against the fence, before the parts are cut. The Store prices the time.
