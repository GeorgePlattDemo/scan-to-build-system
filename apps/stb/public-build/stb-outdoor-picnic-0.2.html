STEP 1 OF 6 · YOUR IDEA page 2 of 7 · make it yours

Make it yours

A plan tells you what to cut and at what angle. Anything beyond that is your addition, and additions are made here. Each one you add becomes a setting for this job; the bench turns it, and the Store says whether it can be done.

What the plan gives you

Add to it

What it needs: which parts (every slat, table and seat), which ends (both ends of each slat, the same angle, as the Store cuts a package), and what angle, which you set on the bench.

Where it’s made: here. The bench only turns it; it never adds one.

Who decides: the Store. Its reference cell declares which angles it cuts (the Store’s envelope). This page doesn’t check it; an angle the Store can’t cut comes back refused, with its reason.

What it needs: how far from each end of the slats, where across the board, and whether you want one in the middle too. You set those on the bench. Each hole is marked with a 3/16 in spot; you drill it.

Why they’re yours: neither plan gives hole locations. Both say to pre-drill, but no measurements. These holes are your addition, never the plan’s.

Who decides: the Store says whether it can spot them.
