Where do the holes go? The plans don’t say. Neither plan publishes hole locations. Ana White says “predrill and countersink”; MyOutdoorPlans names pilot holes and carriage bolts, with no measurements. So no holes are drawn or sent until you place them. If a plan publishes them later, they can come from the plan.

Each board is drawn to its length, with its ends as cut. Spot holes marks a 3/16 in drill spot where you say; you drill. Add a decorative cut angles both ends of a square-cut board. The Store says whether it can do each one; some won’t work, and it says why.
