Cut it to length. Put a hole in it. That’s a whole project and a perfectly good reason to be here. Everything below is the same idea with more in it.

More ways we can help, easiest first.

  1. Add pieces as you go. One board, then the next. You don’t have to know the whole thing up front.
  2. Just tell us in plain words. Say what you’re after and we’ll write down what we heard. You check it.
  3. Type the numbers. Lengths, widths, where the holes go. We draw it back so you can see it.
  4. Send a scan or a photo. Phone, laser, a picture of the wall. Even a napkin sketch.
  5. Draw it here. Boxes at right angles, which is most of what wood is. Enough for a bench or a frame.
  6. Bring what you already have. A cut list, a PDF, a CAD export. Keep working in the tool you know — we’ll take what it gives us.

  1. Cut it. Three cuts along the one board — a 30° angle each — make the two parts.
  2. Drill it. One spot, on location, centered in each part.
  3. Keep the stub. Two matching X-brace parts, and the rest of the board comes back to you.
