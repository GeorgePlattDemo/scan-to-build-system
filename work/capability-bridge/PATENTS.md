## How this bench uses them

- Claims, specification, and figures are primary wording.
- Existing mappings and Atlas text are navigation aids only.
- Correspondence does not establish installed capability, commissioning, safety, or a legal conclusion.
- Exemplary embodiments are not a mandatory MVP.
- Machine Build 1 and the dimensional *reference model* may intentionally implement less than a figure shows. Record that narrowing when the model is filled.

## When to open a grant

Open the relevant PDF before writing or changing:

- stock support, fence/reference, restraint, controlled feed, sawing, drilling, milling;
- definition-to-execution packet relationships;
- any sentence that says a Store offering corresponds to a disclosed cell.

Then classify the relationship with the alignment gate. Do not invent a third paraphrase and treat it as the grant.
