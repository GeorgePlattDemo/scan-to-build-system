This folder is the daily bench.

## Standing pre-instruction

Before trying a new part against the application, follow [`TRIAL-PROTOCOL.md`](TRIAL-PROTOCOL.md) and log the row in [`TRIAL-LOG.md`](TRIAL-LOG.md).

Do not restate that protocol in chat. Use it.

## Objective

Give Store 1 two reference capabilities it can name, describe, and turn into machine-neutral instructions:

1. **Dimensional model** — finished-length work on dimensional stock.
2. **Sheet model (beginning stage)** — rectangular blank family on sheet stock.

## Language gate

1. Name the owning layer: Application, Store, Governed, Machine, or Research.
2. Verb matches evidence: `IMPLEMENTED` / `REFERENCE` / `DOCUMENTED` / `PLANNED` / `NOT CLAIMED`.
3. Do not move authority across a wall.
4. Current names only.
5. Review-era exhibit names stay out of current files.

**NO BLOOD ON WOOD.**
