# Store 1 Expansion Checklist

Future Store 1 expansion should be built as bounded additions to the Store owner layer.

Before a new Store fact is treated as current, identify:

- offering / SKU identity;
- material class and stock form;
- source and assertion basis;
- availability / inventory status and freshness;
- capability required;
- whether the capability is reference, commissioned, or measured;
- economic basis;
- special-order route if local stock cannot satisfy the requirement;
- fulfillment implication;
- explicit unresolved/refusal cases.

Do not derive Store truth from machine CAD, controller configuration, or old public-demo heuristics.
