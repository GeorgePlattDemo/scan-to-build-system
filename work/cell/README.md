# Research Cell Work Surface

**Status:** planned post-app build surface  
**Current physical cell:** not commissioned  
**Exact useful bounded task:** unresolved by design

This folder is the working home for the point where the dimensional and sheet machine tracks become one bounded research cell.

The goal is not to prove that every project needs two machines. The goal is to establish enough real capability to learn whether a useful local two-machine cell exists, what work it should perform, and what Store/cell architecture the evidence supports.

## Cell entry condition

Do not call two pieces of equipment a useful Scan-to-Build cell merely because they are adjacent or networked.

A cell build should eventually identify:

- a bounded task or project-component family;
- dimensional-stock role, if any;
- sheet-stock role, if any;
- common identity and part relationship;
- material handoff or convergence point;
- Store-visible capability declaration;
- local machine programs / lowering owners;
- operator responsibilities;
- physical safety boundary;
- staging / labeling / outcome relationship;
- measurable success and refusal criteria.

## Existing stage relationship

The existing Store/Cell Stage 1–4 document controls evidence-stage language.

- Current app consumes Stage-2 Store Zero reference capability.
- A genuinely physical pilot cell with workholding, guarding, safety-rated controls, interlocks, commissioning and measured behavior is Stage-3 evidence under that existing vocabulary.
- Internal mechanical build phases may use their own qualified names and numbers; they do not rewrite the Store/cell stages.

## Candidate cell questions

The first research task should be chosen because it teaches something, not because it makes the most impressive demo.

Useful questions include:

- Does the task genuinely require or benefit from both material forms/machines?
- Can the requirement remain machine-neutral until local lowering?
- What information must Store 1 know before routing the work?
- What information is machine-local and should remain hidden from Store?
- Does the task expose a real capability gap worth solving?
- Can failure/refusal be measured cleanly?
- Does the result teach anything about labor, material handling, yield, cycle time, operator burden or economics?

## Non-claims

This work surface does not establish:

- production authorization;
- remote Cycle Start;
- a commissioned D-001 or S-001;
- physical safety certification;
- a final tandem machine architecture;
- a Store capability declaration merely because a mechanical design exists;
- that the eventual useful task is already known.

## Relationship to patents

Patent correspondence may help identify tandem functions, dimensional/sheet roles, local computer/controller relationships, labeling, secondary operations and material flow.

Correspondence is not a construction drawing or runtime authority.

The engineering surface owns the detailed correspondence. This cell surface should carry only enough of it to explain the selected research task and the unbroken information-to-physical chain.
